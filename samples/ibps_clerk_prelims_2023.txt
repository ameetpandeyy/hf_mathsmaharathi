Subject: Numerical Ability

Q.1) Simplify: 16 * 15 - 140 = ?
A) 100
B) 110
C) 90
D) 120
Ans: A
Solution: 16 * 15 = 240. 240 - 140 = 100.

Q.2) What is 35% of 400?
A) 120
B) 140
C) 150
D) 130
Ans: B
Solution: (35/100) * 400 = 140.

Q.3) A shopkeeper marks goods 40% above cost price of ₹500 and offers a 10% discount. What is the selling price?
A) ₹630
B) ₹650
C) ₹600
D) ₹620
Ans: A
Solution: Marked Price = 500 * 1.4 = 700. Selling Price = 700 * 0.9 = ₹630.

Q.4) What will come in place of (?) in the series: 4, 9, 16, 25, ?
A) 36
B) 49
C) 35
D) 30
Ans: A
Solution: The series is squares of natural numbers: 2^2, 3^2, 4^2, 5^2, 6^2 = 36.

Q.5) The ratio of milk and water in a 60-liter mixture is 2 : 1. How much water should be added to make the ratio 1 : 1?
A) 20 liters
B) 15 liters
C) 10 liters
D) 25 liters
Ans: A
Solution: Milk = 40L, Water = 20L. For 1:1, water must equal 40L. Added water = 40 - 20 = 20 liters.
