State Bank of India
Probationary Officers Preliminary Examination 2023
Subject: Quantitative Aptitude

Q.1) What will come in place of question mark (?): 45% of 600 + 130 = ?
A) 400
B) 410
C) 390
D) 420
Ans: A
Solution: 45% of 600 = 270. 270 + 130 = 400.

Q.2) Find the roots of the quadratic equation: x^2 - 7x + 12 = 0.
A) 3, 4
B) 2, 5
C) -3, -4
D) 1, 6
Ans: A
Solution: x^2 - 4x - 3x + 12 = 0 => (x-4)(x-3)=0 => roots are 3 and 4.

Q.3) Divide ₹3500 between A and B in the ratio 4 : 3. What is A's share?
A) ₹1500
B) ₹2000
C) ₹1800
D) ₹2100
Ans: B
Solution: A's share = (4/7) * 3500 = 2000.

Q.4) A man travels from town A to town B at 40 km/h and returns at 60 km/h. What is his average speed?
A) 50 km/h
B) 48 km/h
C) 52 km/h
D) 45 km/h
Ans: B
Solution: Average speed = 2xy/(x+y) = 2*40*60 / 100 = 48 km/h.

Q.5) The average age of 5 boys is 16 years. If a new boy aged 22 joins, what is the new average age?
A) 17 years
B) 18 years
C) 17.5 years
D) 16.5 years
Ans: A
Solution: Total sum = 5 * 16 = 80. New sum = 80 + 22 = 102. New average = 102 / 6 = 17 years.
